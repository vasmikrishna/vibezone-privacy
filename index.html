Vibezone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Vibezone organises casual, adults-only social parties in Bangalore. This page explains what happens to your details when you register your interest through our sign-up form on Facebook or Instagram.

What we collect

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number
  • Your answer to whether you are 18 or older

Why we collect it

We use your details only to call you about Vibezone parties and to confirm that you are an adult. Our events are for people aged 18 and over.

Who handles your details

The form runs on Meta's lead ads tools, and the ads are placed through the Varchas store advertising account, so the form shows that name. Meta also handles your information under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your details.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to contact you about our parties, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to show, correct or delete your details, at any time. Tell us when we call you, or message the vibezone_org Page on Facebook.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
